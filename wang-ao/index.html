Biography

Ao Wang is a Ph.D. student in Medical Technology at the Institute of Medical Technology, Peking University Health Science Center, and a member of the AIMI Lab. His research focuses on CT image reconstruction and computational imaging, with particular interests in quantum CT, QUBO models, neural radiance fields, and computed laminography. Before joining Peking University, he received his B.Eng. degree from North China University of Water Resources and Electric Power and his M.Eng. degree from Chongqing University.
