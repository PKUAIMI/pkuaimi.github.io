AIMI NEWS / 02

我组论文获《Physics in Medicine & Biology》接收！

近日，Xihao Wu、Jiacheng Liu、Haijun Yu、Andreas Maier 和 Yixing Huang 合作完成的论文《Self-Cascade Latent Schrödinger Bridge for CT Field-of-View Extension》获《Physics in Medicine & Biology》接收。该研究提出自级联潜空间薛定谔桥（SCL-SB），结合共享权重的两轮自级联机制与频率感知残差机制，用于恢复截断 CT 中缺失的外周解剖结构，并兼顾解剖细节恢复与推理效率。热烈祝贺各位作者！

Our Paper Receives Provisional Acceptance from Physics in Medicine & Biology!

The manuscript “Self-Cascade Latent Schrödinger Bridge for CT Field-of-View Extension,” authored by Xihao Wu, Jiacheng Liu, Haijun Yu, Andreas Maier, and Yixing Huang, has been accepted by Physics in Medicine & Biology. The study introduces a self-cascade latent Schrödinger bridge (SCL-SB) that combines a two-round shared-weight cascade with a frequency-aware residual mechanism to recover missing peripheral anatomy in truncated CT images while maintaining efficient inference. Congratulations to all the authors!

祝贺钟祚栋同学荣获 2025 年度宝钢优秀学生奖！宝钢教育奖由宝钢于 1990 年出资设立，由教育部主管，是全国最具知名度的教育奖项之一，覆盖全国 31 个省区 120 余所高校，每年在全国范围内评选约 500 名优秀学生。在北京科技大学，该奖项面向全校所有专业、所有年级评选，每年仅有 2 名本科生获奖，是学校最具分量的本科生荣誉之一。钟祚栋是北京科技大学自动化学院自动化专业的大四本科生，即将加入本课题组开展研究生阶段的学习。学校官方微信公众号已对此进行报道：https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/BIi0fDSpUGNLGTRJJCXMlg。我们向钟祚栋同学取得的优异成绩表示热烈祝贺，并期待他加入课题组后取得更加出色的研究成果。


Congratulations to Zuodong Zhong on receiving the 2025 Baosteel Outstanding Student Award! At the University of Science and Technology Beijing (USTB), the award is selected university-wide across all majors and grade levels, with only two undergraduate recipients granted annually — making it one of USTB’s most competitive student honors. AIMI NEWS / 15

Congratulations to Hongrun Dong on completing his master’s thesis and starting a new position at United Imaging Healthcare as a Clinical Application Specialist (CAS).

Congratulations to Hongrun Dong on completing his master’s thesis and starting a new position at United Imaging Healthcare as a Clinical Application Specialist (CAS). His M.Sc. thesis is entitled “Deep Learning for Cone-Beam CT Field-of-View Extension.” The thesis was supervised by Yixing Huang, together with Florian Putz and Andreas Maier. This work focuses on projection-domain completion for truncated CBCT projections to improve downstream reconstruction quality. Best wishes to Hongrun for a great start and continued success in his new role.

祝贺董洪润顺利完成硕士毕业论文并入职联影医疗（United Imaging Healthcare），担任临床应用专家（CAS）。其硕士论文题为《Deep Learning for Cone-Beam CT Field-of-View Extension》，由黄益星老师指导（并与 Florian Putz、Andreas Maier 共同指导）。论文围绕锥束CT（CBCT）投影截断导致的视野受限问题，研究投影域补全与外延（projection completion/outpainting）以提升后续重建质量。祝董洪润在新岗位工作顺利、持续成长。
