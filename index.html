About the lab

Meet our people →

The Artificial Intelligence for Medical Imaging (AIMI) Lab at the Institute of Medical Technology, Peking University Health Science Center, has been newly established. The lab is dedicated to advancing both fundamental research and clinical translation of artificial intelligence in medical imaging, with a strong focus on precision medicine.

Its research areas include CT image reconstruction, MRI-based tumor segmentation, image-guided radiation oncology, patient prognosis and survival analysis, digital pathology, and computer-vision-driven medical image generation.

AIMI strives to develop cutting-edge intelligent imaging technologies to support disease diagnosis, treatment planning, and therapy assessment.
