Yixing Huang (黄益星)

Huang Yixing is currently an Assistant Professor and Full Research Professor at the Institute of Medical Technology, Peking University Health Science Center. He received his Bachelor’s degree from Peking University in 2013, and his Master’s and Doctoral degrees from Friedrich-Alexander-University Erlangen-Nuremberg in Germany in 2016 and 2020, respectively. His research primarily focuses on cone-beam CT reconstruction and image analysis, as well as medical image analysis in tumor radiotherapy, including limited-angle reconstruction, field-of-view extension, MRI brain metastasis detection and segmentation, large language model-assisted decision-making, and survival prediction for cancer patients. To date, he has published over 50 academic papers and holds 3 patents, with 30 journal and conference papers as first author or corresponding author, including top medical imaging journals TMI and MedIA, top conference MICCAI, and the Red Journal and Green Journal of radiation oncology. He leads the National Natural Science Foundation of China Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas), the National Natural Science Foundation of China General Program, the Capital Health Development Research Special Youth Key Research Project, and the Beijing Natural Science Foundation Joint Fund Cultivation Project.
