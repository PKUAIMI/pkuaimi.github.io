Meng Li (李萌) is currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Institute of Medical Technology, Peking University Health Science Center, working under the supervision of Dr. Yixing Huang. She obtained her Ph.D. through the joint doctoral program between Northeastern University and the University of Pennsylvania. Her research primarily focuses on developing multimodal MRI-based methodologies to assess brain pathologies and identify potential biomarkers associated with neuropsychiatric disorders. She has conducted some innovative research in the field of brain science and published a number of peer-reviewed works.
