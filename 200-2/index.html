Haijun Yu (余海军)

Haijun Yu is a Boya Postdoctoral Fellow at the Institute of Medical Technology, Peking University Health Science Center, with his collaborating supervisor being Asst. Prof. Yixing Huang. He obtained his doctoral degree from Chongqing University in September 2024. He conducted research as a joint-training doctoral student at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg, Germany in 2023 under the sponsorship of the China Scholarship Council (CSC). His main research areas include linear CT technology and systems, intelligent CT image reconstruction and analysis. He has published 35 academic papers with 13 as first/corresponding author in SCI journals such as Applied Mathematical Modelling, IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement, IEEE Transactions on Radiation and Plasma Medical Sciences, Signal Processing, Optics Express.
